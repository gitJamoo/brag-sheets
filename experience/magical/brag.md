## 3. Accomplishments
1. **Workflow review agent.** Reviews every new piece of work posted to customer workflows, checks it for bugs and regressions, and proposes a patch when it catches one. It moved the team's engineers from implementing changes to reviewing them.
2. **Autonomous fixing agent.** Scans the work the workflows handle, catches real-world issues in live customer automations, and fixes them for customers fully autonomously, finding them before customers do. It raised real-world accuracy to **95%**.
3. **Both agents in production.** Both run on all automations, every day.
4. **Model switch (~$450K a year).** Proposed and carried through a move to cheaper, improved agent models with no regressions, saving an estimated $450K a year.
5. **Evals and benchmarks engine.** Rewrote the evals and benchmarking engine from the ground up. It gives the team the regression checks that made the model switch safe, flags new models that bring cost and time savings as soon as they are released so the team stays on the cutting edge, and opens the door to fine-tuning and broader benchmarking.
6. **Branching and versioning.** Implemented a branching and versioning system for workflows, modeled closely on git.
